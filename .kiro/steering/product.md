# Product

Yokai Kanji is a web game for learning JLPT N5 kanji.

## Player

A beginner Japanese learner who wants short quiz sessions. The player knows some hiragana and wants to learn kanji meaning and reading.

## Core loop

Answer a quiz question. A correct answer earns ofuda and XP. A wrong answer returns later in the same session. Every 10 correct answers in a row unlocks a new yokai.

## Quiz modes

Meaning quiz: show a kanji, pick the correct English meaning from 4 choices.
Reading quiz: show a kanji, pick the correct hiragana reading from 4 choices.

## Collection

Each unlocked yokai has a name, a one sentence story in English, and the kanji it represents. The collection screen shows unlocked and locked yokai.

## Visual theme

Costume Contest theme: a dark Japanese night, lanterns, fog, torii gates. The theme is functional. Lanterns show progress. Ofuda show score. Yokai are the reward. Text must stay readable. Contrast must stay high enough for study.

## Language

All interface text, documentation, comments, and commit messages are in English. Kanji, hiragana, and romaji are study data, not interface language. Example words keep their Japanese form plus romaji and an English translation.

## Out of scope

Accounts, online leaderboard, stroke writing, audio, N4 content.
